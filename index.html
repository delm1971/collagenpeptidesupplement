Collagen 101

What are collagen peptide supplements?

Collagen peptides are fragments of collagen protein created through hydrolysis. They are commonly sold as powders, capsules, and flavored mixes. Different products vary in source, ingredients, and serving size.

✧

Skin & collagen

Some trials suggest modest improvements in certain skin measures, but study quality varies and results are not guaranteed.

◈

Joint wellness

Research has explored collagen for joint discomfort. Evidence remains mixed, and supplements are not substitutes for medical care.

◎

Protein & nutrition

Collagen is a protein, but it is not a complete protein source. Consider it in the context of your overall diet.

The essentials

5 things to check before buying

1. Collagen source

Check whether collagen is bovine, marine, poultry-derived, or from another source—especially if you have allergies or dietary restrictions.

2. Amount per serving

Compare grams of collagen per serving and the number of servings in each package.

3. Ingredient list

Look for sweeteners, flavors, allergens, and other active ingredients you may or may not want.

4. Independent testing

Prefer transparent manufacturers that provide credible quality testing information.

5. Cost per serving

Compare the actual cost per serving rather than judging only by the package price.

Bonus: realistic claims

Be cautious of products promising dramatic or guaranteed results, especially medical or anti-aging claims.

Frequently asked questions

Collagen questions, answered

What is the difference between collagen and collagen peptides?

Collagen peptides are collagen proteins broken into smaller pieces through hydrolysis, typically making them easier to mix into foods and drinks.

Is marine collagen better than bovine collagen?

Neither source is universally best. They differ in origin and may differ in composition. Compare product quality, allergens, and your needs rather than relying on broad superiority claims.

How much collagen should I take?

There is no single universally appropriate dose. Research uses varying amounts and formulations. Review the product label and ask a qualified clinician about your situation, especially if you have health conditions or take medications.

Are collagen supplements suitable for everyone?

No. Some contain fish, bovine, or other animal-derived ingredients. Check allergen information and consult a healthcare professional when appropriate.
