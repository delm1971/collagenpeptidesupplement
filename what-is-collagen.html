What Is Collagen? A Beginner’s Guide

Collagen is a family of structural proteins that helps provide strength and organization to connective tissues.

Where collagen is found

Collagen is abundant in skin, tendons, ligaments, bone, and cartilage, with different types serving different structural roles.

Types I, II, and III

Type I is common in skin and bone; type II is prominent in cartilage; type III occurs in various tissues alongside type I.

How the body makes collagen

Cells synthesize collagen using amino acids and nutrients such as vitamin C. A balanced diet supports normal tissue maintenance.

What happens with aging

Collagen production and remodeling change with age and environmental exposures, including ultraviolet radiation.

Does eating collagen replace lost collagen?

Digestion breaks dietary proteins into amino acids and peptides. Supplementation does not directly deposit collagen into skin or joints.
